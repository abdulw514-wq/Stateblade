FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Kick channel search — FAQ

Can I search Kick channels by keyword for free?

Yes. StateBlade's Kick channel search is completely free and requires no account. Type any keyword or category and get a ranked list of top Kick channels in that niche.

What data does StateBlade show for Kick channels?

StateBlade shows follower counts, live viewer counts (for channels currently streaming), category/game, and whether a channel is live right now — all pulled from the official Kick API.

Does StateBlade support YouTube and Twitch too?

Yes. StateBlade supports YouTube niche research, Twitch channel search, Kick channel search, and Bluesky account search — all free, all in one place.

How is Kick different from Twitch for streamers?

Kick is a newer streaming platform known for more permissive content policies and a higher revenue share for streamers (95/5 split vs Twitch's 50/50). It's grown rapidly since 2022 and has attracted many streamers looking for an alternative.
