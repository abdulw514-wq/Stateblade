Twitch's own discovery tools are limited for competitive research. StateBlade lets you search any keyword — a game title, category, or topic — and instantly surfaces the leading streamers competing in that space.

Whether you're a new streamer trying to find a less-crowded game to start in, or an established creator doing competitive research, StateBlade gives you the data you need in seconds.

How to find Twitch channels by keyword

  • Go to stateblade.com and select the Twitch tab.
  • Type any keyword — a game like "Valorant", a category like "Just Chatting", or a topic like "speedrunning".
  • Hit Scan niche to see the top Twitch channels competing for that keyword, ranked by stats.
  • Compare streamers by viewer counts, follower numbers, and activity frequency.
  • Use the data to find your entry point — where there's audience demand but less saturation.

Tip for new streamers: Search a game you love and look at the follower counts of the top channels. If the leader has 500K followers and everyone else drops off sharply below 10K, the niche is winner-take-most. Look for games where several channels in the 5K–50K range are growing — that's a healthier competitive environment for a new streamer.
