What StateBlade does that SocialBlade doesn't

The core difference is the research direction. SocialBlade is channel-first: you start by knowing which channel you want to look up. StateBlade is niche-first: you start with a topic or keyword and discover the channels.

This makes StateBlade far more useful for:

  • Pre-launch niche research: If you're deciding whether to start a channel on "van life" vs "solo travel", StateBlade shows you the competitive landscape for both in seconds. SocialBlade can't help until you already know which channels to look up.
  • Discovering competitors you didn't know existed: StateBlade surfaces channels competing in your niche that you might never have found manually.
  • Keyword and tag research: StateBlade shows the exact video tags top channels use. This is the closest thing to free competitor keyword research available for YouTube creators.
  • Niche-wide competitive analysis: Instead of looking up 10 channels one by one, StateBlade gives you all of them in a single ranked view.

What SocialBlade does that StateBlade doesn't

  • Historical tracking: SocialBlade's strength is showing you how a channel's subscriber count changed over weeks, months, and years. If you want to see whether a competitor had a big spike after a collab or viral video, SocialBlade is the tool for that.
  • Revenue estimates: SocialBlade calculates estimated monthly revenue ranges based on view counts and typical CPM rates. These are rough estimates, but they're not something StateBlade provides.
  • Daily subscriber counts: SocialBlade tracks subscribers gained or lost each day, which is useful for watching the effect of specific events on a channel's growth.

Frequently asked questions

Is StateBlade really free?

Yes — StateBlade is completely free to use, with no account required. It uses the official YouTube Data API and has no paid tiers or feature paywalls.

Is StateBlade a SocialBlade competitor?

They serve different primary use cases. SocialBlade is primarily a historical tracking tool for individual channels. StateBlade is primarily a niche research tool that lets you search by keyword and compare multiple channels at once. Many creators use both.

Why can't SocialBlade search channels by keyword?

SocialBlade is built around individual channel lookup — you need to already know the channel name or URL. StateBlade is built the other way around: you start with a topic or keyword and discover which channels are competing for it.

Does StateBlade show historical data?

No — StateBlade shows a snapshot of current stats pulled from the YouTube Data API, updated every few hours. It's designed for competitive research and niche analysis, not for long-term trend tracking of individual channels.
