Frequently asked questions

How do I find YouTube channels by keyword for free?

Type any keyword into StateBlade and click "Scan niche." It queries the YouTube Data API and returns a ranked list of channels competing for that term — with subscriber counts, view stats, and upload frequency for each. It's free and requires no account.

Is this a free YouTube channel analytics tool?

StateBlade is a free YouTube niche research and channel stats checker. It shows subscriber counts, total views, and upload frequency for any set of channels matching a keyword — pulled from the official YouTube Data API. It's focused on competitive and niche research rather than your own channel analytics (use YouTube Studio for that).

Can I check YouTube channel subscriber counts without an account?

Yes. StateBlade requires no account, login, or sign-up. Go to stateblade.com, search any niche, and subscriber counts are shown immediately for every result.

How accurate is the YouTube channel data?

StateBlade uses the official YouTube Data API — the same data source YouTube itself uses. Stats are cached for a few hours to stay within the API's free quota, so figures are current within that window.
